Release notes

What changed in each version of {{productNameTM}}, newest first. Updates arrive through the App Store, and everything you have built stays on your iPad when you update.

Version 1.0.2

12 November 2026

Importing songs is easier, especially if you keep your backing tracks as loose files.

  • Import a folder of loose backing tracks and each one becomes its own song. Files named like "Song L" and "Song R" stay together as one song
  • If a folder is read the wrong way, choose One song, By name or Each file, and see the songs it will make before you save
  • Stereo WAV files from most audio software now import correctly. Many 24-bit stereo files used to fail
  • When a file cannot be imported, you are told which one and what to try, instead of an error code
  • After an import, you can leave the songs that did not save rather than having to try again or cancel

Version 1.0.1

11 November 2026

Fixes the screenshots on the App Store page, which appeared rotated on iPad. Nothing in the app itself changed.

Version 1.0

9 November 2026

The first release. Audio, click, MIDI, video and lights, run from one iPad.

  • Multitrack backing tracks, up to 16 per song, each sent to any output on your audio interface
  • A separate mix for every output, so front of house, monitors and in-ears each hear what they need. The click can go to the drummer's ears and nowhere else
  • Setlists with songs that wait for you or run straight into the next, and gaps where you want them
  • Showtime: a big Play bar, cue the next song and play it, with the setlist and the state of your rig in view
  • Video and lighting lined up against the audio in one window, frame by frame, with undo and Save
  • MIDI patch changes on time with the song
  • A Line Check before the show, covering audio, screen, MIDI and lights, with a link to fix anything that needs it
  • Charts and lyrics that follow the song, and a stand page that shows every player their part on their own device, with nothing to install
  • A phone remote to play, stop, cue and mix from anywhere on the stage
  • Import whole folders of songs at once, and rename and route many songs in one grid
  • Runs on iPhone too, and upright on iPad if you prefer
  • Back up the whole rig to one file
  • Free for 28 days, then one purchase. No subscription
