The commitment

What We Ask of a Tester

None of this is onerous, and all of it matters. It is the difference between a beta that finds problems in a rehearsal room and one that finds them in front of an audience.

  • Put it through a rehearsal before you trust it at a show, every time there is a new build.
  • Tell us when something goes wrong, even when it looks small or you are not sure it was the app.
  • Send the report from the device while it is fresh, rather than describing it from memory a week later.
  • Say what your rig is: the iPad, the interface, anything on MIDI, the screen, the DMX node.
  • Expect questions back, and the odd request to try one specific thing and tell us what it did.
  • Never risk a real show on unfinished software. If in doubt, run the show the way you already know works.

Testing

Once You Are In

Install TestFlight from the App Store, accept the invitation on the iPad you gig with, and the build appears there. New builds arrive on their own. Each one carries a short note saying what changed and what we would like tried, so read that first and let it steer you.

What is worth your time

  • Rehearse a real set with it before you play one
  • Use your own rig: your interface, your screen, your pedals
  • Try the thing this build asks about, then try to break it
  • Tell us when something is confusing, not only when it is broken

What to send

  • Something went wrong on the iPad: send a report from the app
  • Anything else: the feedback form on this site
  • One thing per report reads better than five in a paragraph
  • What you were doing matters more than what you think caused it

Do not take a beta build to a show you are being paid for without a tested fallback. That is not modesty about the software, it is what any sensible person does with anything new, and it is the first thing we would say to a friend.

Reporting

How to Send a Report

The app can send a report itself, with what it was doing at the time already filled in. That is always better than a description written from memory, because it carries the detail nobody remembers to mention.

Anything that is not a report from the device, a feature you want, a screen that made no sense, a piece of gear that would not talk to it, comes through the form on this site instead.
